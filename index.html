XAirports

Seamless X-Plane Companion. Set COM and NAV frequencies, autopilot parameters, and transponder codes without pausing your flight. Access a comprehensive database of over 34,000 airports, runways, ATC, ILS, and VOR stations. Display, save, and print airport charts from multiple sources—all from a native macOS app.

Instant Frequency Selection

Click to select and send COM/NAV frequencies directly to X-Plane. No memorizing numbers or fiddling with knobs. Stay immersed in your flight.

Comprehensive Airport Database

34,000 airports, 42,000 runways, 22,000 ATC, 3,500 ILS, and 9,500 VOR. Filter by name, ICAO, or runway length. Sort by distance to find the nearest suitable landing spot for any aircraft.

Airport Charts and Maps

View, print, and save SID/STAR charts from over a dozen sources. Overlay airports, nav aids, and your aircraft on a moving map with 13 map styles. Import custom scenery for precision.

Quick Autopilot and Transponder

Set autopilot parameters and transponder codes in seconds. Supports X-Plane 9.70 and 10—no plugins required.

Effortless Workflow

1. Search and Filter

Enter airport name, ICAO, or filter by runway length. Airports sort automatically by distance to your position.

2. Select and Send

Click COM/NAV frequency, autopilot, or transponder setting. Data transmits instantly to X-Plane.

3. Access Charts and Maps

Load SID/STAR charts or view on a customizable moving map. Save or print as needed.

4. Stay in the Air

No pauses, no distractions. Fly with full situational awareness.
